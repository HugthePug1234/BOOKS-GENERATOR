Turn your ideas into a novel! Create characters, design your story, and generate a book in minutes.

Get Started

Build your book in these easy steps:

  • Create Characters: Add at least 2 characters with details like name, age, and relationships.
  • Design Your Book: Set the genre, setting, tone, themes, and other details.
  • Generate a Plot: Create a story outline based on your characters and settings.
  • Preview the Structure: Review a summary of your book’s chapters (optional).
  • Generate the Book: Produce a complete novel (100–400 pages, 10–100 chapters), view it on-screen, and download it as a Markdown file.
